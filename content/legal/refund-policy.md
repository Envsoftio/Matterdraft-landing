## 2. No voluntary refunds

**All authorised purchases are final, and we do not provide voluntary refunds, credits, exchanges or prorated reimbursements, to the fullest extent permitted by applicable law.**

Subject to Section 3, this means we do not refund a purchase simply because:

- You change your mind or no longer need the product.
- You use it less than expected, stop using it or leave part of a paid term unused.
- You decide to use another product or your business circumstances change.
- You prefer a feature or integration that was not included or promised in your order.
- Your system does not meet compatibility requirements that were accurately disclosed before purchase.
- You cancel a properly authorised subscription after the current paid period has begun.

Read the product description, supported systems, licence scope, total price and any renewal terms before paying. Use a demonstration or trial if one is offered; this policy does not promise that a free trial is available.

## 3. Mandatory rights and billing corrections

**Nothing in this policy excludes a refund, withdrawal right, cancellation right, price reduction, repair, replacement, compensation or other remedy that applicable law requires.** We will not use the no-voluntary-refunds rule to avoid an obligation we are legally required to meet.

Depending on the applicable law and circumstances, this may include:

- A defective or deficient product or service, or a material failure to match the features or description agreed at purchase.
- Failure to deliver the purchased entitlement, or qualifying late delivery, where a remedy is required.
- A confirmed duplicate, unauthorised or incorrectly calculated charge, including a charge taken after a valid cancellation when it should not have been taken.
- A statutory withdrawal or cancellation right that remains available for the transaction.
- A refund or other remedy ordered by a competent authority, or required by binding purchase-channel terms.

The applicable remedy may depend on the nature of the problem and the law. We will not insist on troubleshooting, repair or another procedure where doing so would unlawfully obstruct a refund you are entitled to receive. Downloading, activating or using the product does not automatically waive a mandatory right.

We will also honour a specific written refund commitment included in your accepted order. A later change to this policy does not remove that commitment.

## 4. Subscription cancellation, if offered

If your order is for a recurring subscription, you may stop future renewal through **[CANCELLATION_METHOD_OR_SUPPORT_EMAIL]**. Submit the cancellation before the next renewal charge is processed. We will confirm the effective cancellation date and handle the request in accordance with applicable law.

If your purchase is a one-time licence, there is no automatic recurring charge unless you separately and expressly authorise one. The licence and update entitlements are those stated in your order.

Where no fixed period applies, we will act within a reasonable period and tell you the expected processing time. The time for a bank or payment provider to display the credit can vary.

We will not deduct an administrative or processing fee where that would reduce an amount the law requires us to return. A refund that cancels a licence or entitlement may end the right to use that entitlement, but it does not transfer ownership of your documents to us or authorise deletion of your local files.

### Primary legal basis checked on 29 October 2026

The no-voluntary-refunds position is a business choice. It cannot remove applicable statutory remedies. India's Consumer Protection Act, 2019 addresses refusal to remedy defective goods or deficient services and authorises consumer remedies. The E-Commerce Rules also contain refund and disclosure duties where they apply. Whether a particular purchaser qualifies as a consumer depends on the facts; a professional product label alone does not decide that question.

- [Consumer Protection Act, 2019](https://consumeraffairs.gov.in/public/upload/files/CP%20Act%202019_1732700731.pdf), especially Sections 2(7), 2(47)(viii) and 39.
- [Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020](https://consumeraffairs.gov.in/public/upload/files/E%20commerce%20rules_1732703966.pdf), especially Rules 4(5), 4(8)–(10), 6(3) and 7(4).
- [Department of Consumer Affairs legislation index](https://consumeraffairs.gov.in/pages/consumer-protection-acts), including the 2026 amendments scheduled to commence on 1 January 2027.
