## 2. Current availability and website demonstrations

MatterDraft is in development for Windows and macOS. Ask about current availability. Website illustrations and interactive examples demonstrate intended workflows; they are not evidence that a production feature has been released.

The current website demonstration does not accept payments, create customer accounts or upload legal documents. Its demo-request form prepares text for you to copy. We do not receive that text through the form in its current configuration. If you later send it to us through email or another enabled contact channel, we will receive the information you choose to send.

Other interactive examples, including editable template fields, sample OCR text and bookmark examples, operate within the page. Use fictional information when exploring them. Closing an on-page dialog does not necessarily clear its contents. Browser autofill, clipboard history, caching and device backups are controlled by your browser, operating system or device settings.

If a contact form is connected to a submission service, we will update the notice presented with that form before it starts sending information. This policy does not authorise undisclosed transmission of information entered into a demonstration.

## 3. Local document processing and connected features

MatterDraft is being developed primarily as a local-first desktop workspace for organising and processing legal documents. For a feature that runs entirely on your device, your source files, recognised text, matter information and generated outputs are handled locally. We do not receive those contents through that local-only operation.

"Local-first" does not mean that every possible feature works without a network connection. For example, a separately offered sharing, synchronisation, support, update or licence-management function may communicate over a network. Before a feature sends document contents or other personal information to us or a service provider, its notice must explain the information involved, its purpose and destination, and the available choice or required authorisation.

Files you place in a cloud-synchronised folder, network drive, third-party backup or another application may be handled by that service under its own terms. This is separate from a local MatterDraft operation.

## 5. Legal documents and information about other people

Legal documents may contain privileged, confidential or sensitive information about clients, witnesses, children and other people. You are responsible for establishing the authority or other lawful basis needed to process that information and for meeting your professional obligations.

Do not include complete case files in an ordinary enquiry. If a support issue requires a sample, first remove information that is unnecessary for the problem and use an approved support method. Tell us about any restrictions before sharing a file.

Sending material to a software provider does not, by itself, create an advocate-client relationship with that provider or guarantee preservation of any legal privilege. Our access to support material is limited to the purpose for which it was provided and any separate lawful requirements.

This policy does not grant us ownership of your documents or permission to publish them. Using confidential matter contents for advertising or model training is outside the purposes described in this policy; any proposed new use would require an appropriate separate notice and lawful authorisation before it begins.
